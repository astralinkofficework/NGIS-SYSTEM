1. Foundational Algebraic Theorems

In single-variable polynomial rings, the roots of degree-two quadratics satisfy the discriminant criterion:

x = (-b ± √(b² - 4ac)) / (2a)

2. Standard Form & Parabolic Extrema

For any quadratic function f(x) = ax² + bx + c where a ≠ 0, the parabola opens upward if a > 0 and downward if a < 0. The coordinate of the vertex lies at h = -b / (2a) and k = f(h).
